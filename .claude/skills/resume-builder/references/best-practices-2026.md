## 1. What gets a career-changer into the interview pile
- Lead with proof you turn data into business decisions, not career story. (BeamJobs/Resume Worded, 2026)
- Quantified achievements in most bullets: only ~30% of data resumes have them. (Jobscan, 100k resumes, 2025)
- Projects get near-equal billing with jobs for career changers. (CV Compiler, 2026)
- Match the posting's exact tool names: SQL ~57% of postings, Python ~52%, Tableau ~40%, Power BI ~38%. (iCareerSolutions, 2025)
- 65% of hiring managers say AI-polished generic phrasing makes skills harder to assess; specificity is the authenticity signal. (Robert Half, Mar 2026)

## 2. AI screeners in 2026
- Contextual achievement sentences outrank keyword lists; stuffing scores LOWER. (Jobscan, 2026)
- Common thresholds: ~80%+ match auto-advances, 60-79% human review, <60% filtered. (TheHireHub, 2026)
- Screeners triangulate resume vs LinkedIn on titles/employers/dates; mismatches flag dishonesty. (Treegarden, 2026)
- Vary metric precision: "cut cycle from 6 hrs to 45 min" beats "improved efficiency 50%". All-round numbers read fabricated. (Treegarden, 2026)

## 3. Summary
- Value summary, never objective: objectives correlate with ~28% lower callbacks for career changers. (Interview Guys, 2026)
- 50-100 words, 3-5 sentences. Strongest number goes IN the summary (6-7 second F-pattern scan). (Ladders eye-tracking)
- Structure: present-tense identity in field, then 2-3 quantified transferable wins, then their business problem.

## 4. Bullets
- Formula: action verb + tool + quantified result + business outcome. 3-5 bullets per recent role, 2-3 older.
- Quantify scope when outcome numbers don't exist: dataset size, users served, cadence. Never invent.
- Fabrication tells interviewers now probe: outsized claims, all-round numbers, no visible mechanism, inflated scope. One indefensible number ends the loop. (Forbes, Mar 2026)
- Rule: only claim numbers whose baseline, method, and timeframe can be reconstructed aloud.

## 5. Skills
- 8-15 items grouped by category; 41% of recruiters weight this section heavily. (CareerKit, 2026)
- Every listed skill must reappear inside a bullet with a result: orphan skills are the #1 stuffing tell.
- Hard skills only in the list; soft skills live inside bullets with scope.

## 6. Self-employment (critical for Ben)
- Give the business its name and a FUNCTIONAL title describing the work; bare "Business Owner" triggers skepticism, sometimes instant rejection. (Distinctive Career Services, 2025)
- Format exactly like a job: company, title, dates, location, achievement bullets.
- Preempt "can he take direction" with evidence of answering to others: clients served concurrently, regulatory compliance, brokerage oversight. Assertion doesn't work; evidence does.
- Honest camp beats concealment camp: ownership visible but not headlined; discovery of masking is fatal.
- Recent-dated certs/coursework answer "will he stay?" implicitly.

## 7. AI fluency
- ~45% of data postings mention AI; AI-skill demand +144% YoY. Omitting it is a gap. (Gloat, Q2 2026)
- Never bare-list AI tools. Workflow evidence only: what was built/accelerated, with verified time/quality outcome. (Resume Genius 2026)
- Include the judgment layer: employers explicitly want evidence you verify AI output.
- Tension: AI fluency helps; an AI-written-LOOKING resume hurts (76% of hiring managers). Specific human voice is the defense.

## 8. Veteran status
- Military service belongs in experience (or Earlier Career) with civilian-translated language and numbers; no separate callout section. Protected-status self-ID happens in application forms, not the resume. (ResumeBuilder/Indeed, 2026)
- Clearance note near the top is standard when relevant.

## 9. Length, format, links
- Two pages for 10+ years: 2.3x callbacks (ResumeGo, 7,712-resume study); ~17% minority still penalize page 2.
- Single column, no tables (failed 5-7 of 8 parsers tested), contact info in body not header/footer, MM/YYYY dates, text-based PDF only. (QuickCV 8-ATS test, 2026)
- Links in contact block with visible labeled URLs; cite the strongest project in a bullet so it gets read even unclicked. No reliable CTR data exists.

## Ship checklist
1. Summary 50-100 words: practicing-analyst framing, 2-3 quantified wins, their problem.
2. Every bullet: verb + tool + number + outcome; every number defensible aloud.
3. Self-employment: named business, functional title, answered-to-others evidence.
4. Skills 8-15, grouped, zero orphans.
5. One AI-workflow bullet with a verified outcome; resume must not read AI-written.
6. Matches LinkedIn exactly on titles/employers/dates.
7. Single column, contact in body, MM/YYYY, text PDF, two pages max.
8. Posting's exact tool tokens present, their sentences absent.
9. Portfolio labeled in contact block AND cited in a bullet.
10. verify_resume.py passes.
